# Device Inspection

## Goal

Before installing Windows or performing any software repair, a complete hardware inspection should be performed. This prevents installation failures and allows the technician to identify existing hardware problems before beginning work.

---

# 1. Discuss the device with the customer

Ask the customer about:

- Current problems
- Previous repairs
- Slow performance
- Random shutdowns
- Blue screens
- Battery problems
- Charging issues
- Gaming performance
- Any important files

Record every important detail.

---

# 2. External inspection

Check the overall physical condition.

Inspect:

- LCD panel
- Hinges
- Keyboard
- Touchpad
- Camera
- Speakers
- USB ports
- HDMI
- LAN port
- Audio jack
- SD Card reader
- Power connector

Look for:

- Cracks
- Broken plastic
- Loose hinges
- Bent ports
- Liquid damage
- Missing screws

---

# 3. Power system

Check that:

- Original charger is available
- Correct charger wattage
- Battery charges normally
- Device does not randomly shut down

A weak charger may reduce performance and cause installation problems.

---

# 4. Cooling system

Check for:

- High temperatures
- Loud fan noise
- Dust buildup
- Poor airflow

If necessary, recommend:

- Internal cleaning
- Fan cleaning
- Thermal paste replacement

Explain the benefits to the customer before opening the device.

---

# 5. Internal inspection (when approved)

If the customer agrees:

Inspect:

- Cooling fan
- Heat sink
- Thermal paste
- SSD
- HDD
- RAM
- Battery condition
- Signs of liquid damage
- Burn marks
- Corrosion

Do not disassemble sealed devices unless necessary and approved.

---

# 6. Recommend optional maintenance

Suggest additional services when appropriate.

Examples:

- Internal cleaning
- Thermal paste replacement
- SSD upgrade
- RAM upgrade
- Battery replacement

Never pressure the customer.

Explain the benefits clearly.

---

# 7. Documentation

Before opening the device:

- Take photos
- Record a short video
- Document existing scratches
- Document existing damage

This protects both the technician and the customer.

---

# Inspection Checklist

- Customer interview completed
- External inspection completed
- Charger checked
- Battery checked
- Cooling inspected
- Ports tested
- LCD checked
- Hinges checked
- Internal inspection approved (if required)
- Photos taken
